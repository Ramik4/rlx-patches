* **Voice over translation (Yandex)**: a player button that plays a Yandex voice-over translation of
  the video (YouTube), next to Morphe's own translation of the captions.
* **Save to device**: videos, audio, thumbnails and subtitles with a quality picker, a library of the
  saved videos and an offline player with SponsorBlock (YouTube, off by default).
* **Quick download menu** for [YTindir](https://github.com/Ramik4/ytindir): qualities with frame rate,
  codec and file size, audio only (M4A, MP3) and the thumbnail, from the download button of YouTube
  and the Download item of YouTube Music.
* **RLX branding**: app icons (red, green, layered), header logos, startup animation and app names,
  next to the original YouTube ones.
* **RLX settings**: the settings in fewer, clearer sections, with *RLX Customizations* for the app
  name, icon, logo, font, theme colors, settings name, language and import/export.
* **RLX default settings**, applied once and kept on updates; *Restore RLX default settings* sets
  everything back to them (channel whitelists, Spoof video streams and the PoToken provider stay).
* **Theme colors**: dark `#0F0F0F` and light `#F0F0F0` from the startup screen on, any other color
  (pure black too) can be selected in the app.
* **About page** with the RLX and Morphe versions, update check, release notes, links and credits.
